# CareConsent for clinical and front-desk staff

English training, build b9229b9. The patients shown are made-up practice patients.

## 01. Finding your way and getting help

Role: All staff

### 00:00:00.000 — The menu and the help box

Welcome to CareConsent. This app records what each patient agrees to about the use of their personal information. The menu on the left only shows the screens you need for your job, so you will not see screens meant for the privacy office. At the top of every screen there is a box called Need help with this screen. Open it to read what the screen is for, how to fill it in, and answers to common questions. There is also a button to watch a short video, like this one.

- Use the menu on the left
- Open Need help with this screen
- Watch the short video if you are stuck

Keep in mind: You only see the screens your job needs.

### 00:00:32.542 — The small i next to each box

When you fill in a form, look for the small round i next to each box. Move your mouse over it, or tap it on a tablet, and a short note explains what to type and why the hospital needs it. Here, the note explains that the date of birth tells us whether the patient is a child. A child needs a parent or guardian to make the choices.

- Find the small i next to a box
- Point at it or tap it
- Read why the box is needed

Keep in mind: If you are unsure, read the note before typing.

## 02. Today's overview

Role: Reception and clinical staff

### 00:00:56.000 — What is waiting for you today

The Overview screen is a quick picture of today's work. The numbers at the top show how many patients are registered, how many have given their choices, and how many are still waiting. Awaiting consent means nobody has asked the patient yet. It does not stop treatment. Use the Register patient button when someone new arrives, or click a patient's name to carry on where you left off.

- Check the numbers at the top
- Register patient for new arrivals
- Click a name to carry on

Keep in mind: Awaiting consent never stops treatment.

## 03. Registering a patient

Role: Reception

### 00:01:22.833 — Find the patient first

Before you register anyone, open the Patient directory and search for the patient's name or hospital number. This avoids creating the same patient twice. The hospital number is the patient's record number from the hospital system. It is not a branch number. If the patient is already listed, click their name instead of registering them again.

- Open Patient directory
- Search by name or hospital number
- Only register if not found

Keep in mind: One patient, one record. Search first.

### 00:01:47.542 — Fill in the registration form

Click Register patient and fill in the form. Type the full name, the hospital number and the date of birth. Choose whose phone it is, then type the ten digit mobile number. If the patient wants messages by email, add their email address and tick the box to say they agreed. Never type an Aadhaar number here. When you are done, click Save and review choices.

- Name, hospital number, date of birth
- Whose phone, then the mobile number
- Save and review choices

Keep in mind: Never type an Aadhaar number.

## 04. Recording the patient's choices

Role: Reception

### 00:02:13.542 — Read the notice with the patient

Next you will see the patient's choices. The notice at the top explains, in the patient's own language, how the hospital will use their information. Read it with the patient, or use the button to have the device read it aloud. Then let the patient tick each use they agree to. Optional uses never affect their treatment. Finally, send the one-time code to the patient's phone and type the code they read back to you.

- Read the notice with the patient
- Let the patient pick each use
- Send the code and type it in

Keep in mind: The patient decides. Never tick for them.

### 00:02:42.500 — The consent register

The Consent register lists every choice that has been recorded, with the date and the person who recorded it. Use it to check what a patient agreed to before you share or use their information. If a patient changes their mind, open their record and choose Withdraw. Withdrawing stops future use. It does not cancel their care.

- Check what was agreed
- Look at the date and recorder
- Use Withdraw if they change their mind

Keep in mind: Withdrawing never cancels care.

## 05. Correcting patient details

Role: Reception

### 00:03:05.792 — Open the patient's record

Mistakes happen. If a phone number, email, date of birth or notice language was typed wrongly, open the patient's record from the Patient directory. You will see their details and every choice they made. Click Edit details to correct them.

- Open the patient from the directory
- Check their details
- Click Edit details

Keep in mind: Fix mistakes here, not on paper.

### 00:03:23.750 — Make the correction

Change only the box that is wrong. Leave the mobile number blank if it is still correct. You can add or change the patient's email, and change the language their notice is shown in. If a new date of birth would make the patient a child, only a privacy officer can save it. Type a short reason for the change, then click Save changes. The change is kept in the record's history.

- Change only what is wrong
- Type a short reason
- Click Save changes

Keep in mind: Every change is kept in the history.

## 06. When a patient asks about their information

Role: Reception

### 00:03:51.208 — Record the patient's request

Patients can ask to see their information, correct it, or have it removed. When a patient asks, open Patient rights and click New request. Find the patient, choose the type of request, and write down what they asked for in their own words. Check their identity as your hospital requires, and note how you checked it. The privacy office will take it from there.

- Patient rights, then New request
- Pick the patient and request type
- Write what they asked and save

Keep in mind: Record it the same day the patient asks.

## 07. Sharing records with another organisation

Role: Reception and clinical staff

### 00:04:17.333 — Record a sharing approval

Before records go to another hospital, a lab or an insurer, write it down here. Open Data sharing and click Record sharing approval. Find the patient, type the name of the organisation, and choose why the records are being shared. Pick only the smallest set of information that is needed. This screen keeps a note of the approval. It does not send the records.

- Data sharing, then Record approval
- Patient, organisation and reason
- Share only what is needed

Keep in mind: This keeps a note. It does not send records.

## 08. For doctors and nurses

Role: Doctors and nurses

### 00:04:44.292 — Your menu

Doctors and nurses see a slightly different menu. You can look up patients and their choices, and you also have Procedure records. You will not see reception tasks such as sharing approvals, or privacy office screens.

- Look up patients and their choices
- Use Procedure records
- Ask reception for other tasks

Keep in mind: Your menu matches your job.

### 00:05:00.625 — Procedure records

Procedure records link a patient to the signed paper or electronic form for a procedure, such as surgery. Click Record procedure evidence, choose the patient, type the procedure name and the doctor, then type the reference number of the signed form. This does not replace the procedure consent form itself. The doctor must still explain the procedure and have the patient sign the approved form.

- Record procedure evidence
- Pick the patient
- Type the signed form's reference

Keep in mind: This does not replace the signed form.

## 09. Your settings

Role: All staff

### 00:05:27.500 — Readiness and settings

Readiness and settings shows your account and how the hospital has set up CareConsent. Here you can switch on a sign-in code app for extra safety, and see the training your hospital has assigned to you. You can change the screen language and the light or dark look from the boxes at the top of every screen.

- Check your account
- Set up your sign-in code app
- See your assigned training

Keep in mind: Never share your password or codes.

## 10. What patients see

Role: Patients

### 00:05:48.000 — The patient's own privacy page

Patients who have an account see their own privacy page. They can check the choices they made, change or withdraw them, send a request about their information, and download a summary. If a patient asks how to do this, show them this page. If they cannot use a phone or computer, they can always ask at the front desk instead.

- Patients check their choices
- They can change or withdraw
- The front desk can always help

Keep in mind: Patients can always ask in person.
