## 02. Roles and the meaning of consent

Role: All learners

### 00:01:52.917 — Understand your workspace role

Different accounts see different navigation. Reception and clinicians work within assigned patient scope; privacy officers manage privacy cases; auditors inspect permitted evidence. An administrator session has no direct patient-read permission. However, directory managers can provision privacy-officer accounts and reset staff credentials, so they remain trusted identity operators. Stronger privileged appointment and recovery approval is still an open production gate. Use your own account, verify hospital and branch, and never borrow another role to bypass a restriction.

- Check the signed-in account
- Identify your hospital and branch
- Use only authorised functions

Keep in mind: Direct role denial does not prove separation from trusted directory administration.

### 00:02:35.417 — Separate data choices from treatment consent

CareConsent records choices about personal information for specific purposes. It also has a separate register for references to clinical procedure evidence. Neither registration, an OTP nor a downloaded data receipt signs a surgery consent form. Clinicians must still complete their approved procedure-specific process. A patient's refusal of optional hospital offers must not be treated as refusal of care or authority to ignore emergency procedures.

- Identify the processing purpose
- Keep clinical forms separate
- Refer treatment questions to clinicians

Keep in mind: Data-processing consent and clinical consent are separate.
